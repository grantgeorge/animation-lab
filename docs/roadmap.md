# Animation Lab roadmap

The goal is an interactive curriculum that teaches filmmakers to **recognize, describe and direct** the qualities they want from animation and from generative AI video. Each lab changes one variable and shows why the result looks or feels different. Each one also writes the matching direction as a copyable prompt.

## The shared pattern

Every lab offers the same three ways in:

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Watch** | The finished animated result, full frame |
| **Inspect** | The technical breakdown: cameras, lights, rigs, curves, passes |
| **Experiment** | The controls: change the mechanics and see the result update live |

Every lab also ends in the same two outputs:
- **A plain-language reading** of what changed and why it reads that way.
- **A deterministic prompt phrase** for AI video models.

Shot Coverage is the first lab built around Watch / Inspect / Experiment. Lens & Framing, Camera Movement and Lighting Studio already have the stage and monitor equivalent, and will adopt the switch when they're next revised.

## Next, in priority order

1. **VFX & Simulation:** a character knocks over a bucket of water as a realistic sim, cartoon 3D and hand-drawn 2D. Start with a lightweight particle system (stylized magic, smoke, falling leaves) rather than a fluid solver. The lesson: physical accuracy is not artistic quality.
2. **Procedural vs Keyframed Animation:** one move authored by an animator, driven by physics, generated by procedural rules or synthesized by AI. Which parts of a shot should a director control?
3. **Stylized Rendering & Materials:** clay → CG → cel → halftone on the same shot. Painterly and cel already exist in Painterly Textures and Animation Camera; this lab would unify them.

Multiplane & Parallax and much of Focus & Depth of Field are now covered by Animation Camera (experiment 3), Environment & Depth and Cinema Camera Families (aperture step). Storyboards & Animatics overlaps with Editing & Rhythm and should be repositioned toward boarding and story-reel timing.

Character Posing & Acting from the proposal is largely covered by lab 5. A silhouette toggle should be added there or in a posing lab.
