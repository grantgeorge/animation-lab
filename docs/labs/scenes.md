# Lens & Framing scenes

The lab ships three scenes that share one sound stage. They use the same rail (x −0.35, lens height 1.47 m), the same 36 × 20.25 mm gate, the same lens kit, and the same Bram plane at z −3. They also share the cast: the two Meshy GLBs walk from set to set. Each scene is a `SceneDescriptor` in `src/labs/lens-and-framing/`:

| Scene | File | Loop | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Doorway (drama) | `config.ts` → `doorwayScene` | 6 s | Matched framing: the doorway grows behind Bram at long lenses |
| The Long Alley (action) | `scenes/alley.ts` | 8 s | Telephoto compression of a sprint toward the lens |
| The Workshop (comedy) | `scenes/workshop.ts` | 6 s | Close quarters; a long lens stacks Pip onto Bram's shoulder |

- **Active scene.** `sceneDescriptor` is a live ES binding that points at the active scene. Only `setActiveScene` (called by the store's `setScene`) changes it, before the lab remounts its scene subtree. Code that must stay on the doorway, such as `/history`, imports `doorwayScene`.
- **Generic set slots.** The engine's set references are named after the doorway set: `lantern`, `doorway`, `backWall` and `tower`. Every scene maps its own props onto them, and `labels` gives the copy and the perspective guide the right words.
- **Per-scene look.** Lighting, fog and the shadow box come from `look`.
- **Performances.** Each scene has one in `src/scene/animation/performances/`: `doorwayWave`, `alleyRun` and `workshopBoo`. They use the same keyframe DSL. The run cycle is generated by `cycles.ts` as ordinary keys.
- **Raised characters.** A character may stand on a riser: the root height is included everywhere (Pip stands on the workshop's loft riser). Framing boxes are absolute world heights.
- **Tests.** `scenes/scenes.test.ts` covers every scene:
  - a shared stage;
  - natural perspective at the default position, for every lens in Fixed;
  - both characters framed at 24 mm;
  - Match on Bram feasible at every preset;
  - valid performances with zero seam velocity.

  It also pins the two lessons. Pip's sprint grows him more than 4.5× at 24 mm matched and less than 2.6× at 85 mm. In the workshop, Pip's apparent size next to Bram is at least 15 % larger at 85 mm matched than at 24 mm.
- **Screenshots.** In `docs/screenshots/scenes/`, regenerated with `node scripts/shots-scenes.mjs`.
